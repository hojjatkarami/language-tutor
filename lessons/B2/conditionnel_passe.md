# The Past Conditional (Le Conditionnel Passé) (B2)

The **conditionnel passé** is used to express regrets, relief, or hypotheses about the past (actions that would have happened under different circumstances). It translates to "would have + past participle".

## How to Form the Past Conditional
It is a compound tense:
> **Auxiliary (avoir/être in Present Conditional) + Past Participle**

*   **avoir** in conditional: *j'aurais, tu aurais, il aurait, nous aurions, vous auriez, ils auraient*
*   **être** in conditional: *je serais, tu serais, il serait, nous serions, vous seriez, ils seraient*

### Examples:
*   *faire*: *J'**aurais** fait* (I would have done)
*   *partir* (uses *être* - requires agreement): *Elle **serait** partie* (She would have left)
