# Hypothesis with 'Si' Clauses (B2)

In French, there are three types of **Si Clauses** (conditional sentences) that follow strict combinations of tenses.

## 1. Type 1: Real / Possible Situations
Expresses something that will happen if a condition is met.
> **Si + Present** $\rightarrow$ **Future**

*   *Si tu **étudies**, tu **réussiras**.* (If you study, you will succeed.)

## 2. Type 2: Hypothetical / Contrary to Present
Expresses what would happen in a hypothetical or unlikely scenario.
> **Si + Imparfait** $\rightarrow$ **Present Conditional**

*   *Si j'**avais** de l'argent, je **voyagerais**.* (If I had money, I would travel.)

## 3. Type 3: Past Impossible / Regret
Expresses what would have happened if a past condition had been met.
> **Si + Plus-que-parfait** $\rightarrow$ **Past Conditional**

*   *Si tu m'**avais dit**, j'**aurais compris**.* (If you had told me, I would have understood.)
