# Conjunctions Requiring the Subjunctive (B2)

Certain conjunctions that connect clauses automatically trigger the subjunctive mood, regardless of doubt or certainty.

## Common Conjunctions
*   **Concession (Although)**: *bien que, quoique*
    *   *Bien qu'il **fasse** froid, je sors.* (Although it's cold, I'm going out.)
*   **Purpose (So that)**: *pour que, afin que*
    *   *Je te le dis **pour que** tu le **saches**.* (I'm telling you so that you know it.)
*   **Time (Before, Until)**: *avant que, jusqu'à ce que*
    *   *Lave-toi les mains **avant que** nous **mangions**.* (Wash your hands before we eat.)
*   **Condition (Provided that)**: *pourvu que, à condition que*
    *   *Tu peux sortir **à condition que** tu **finisses** tes devoirs.* (Provided that you finish...)
