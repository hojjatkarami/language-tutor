# Complex Relative Pronouns (Lequel) (B2)

Unlike simple relative pronouns (qui, que), the pronoun **lequel** is used after prepositions (sur, sous, avec, dans, etc.) and must agree in gender and number with the noun it replaces.

## Forms of Lequel
| Gender/Number | Basic Form | Contracted with **à** | Contracted with **de** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Masc. Sing. | **lequel** | **auquel** | **duquel** |
| Fem. Sing. | **laquelle** | **à laquelle** | **de laquelle** |
| Masc. Plur. | **lesquels** | **auxquels** | **desquels** |
| Fem. Plur. | **lesquelles** | **auxquelles** | **desquelles** |

## Rules of Usage
1.  **After a preposition**: *La maison **dans laquelle** je vis.* (The house in which I live.)
2.  **Contracted forms**: If the preposition is **à** or **de**, use the contracted forms:
    *   *penser à* $\rightarrow$ *L'examen **auquel** je pense.* (The exam I am thinking about.)
    *   *à côté de* $\rightarrow$ *Le parc **à côté duquel** j'habite.* (The park next to which I live.)
