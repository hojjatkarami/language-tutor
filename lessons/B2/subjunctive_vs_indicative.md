# Subjunctive vs. Indicative (B2)

Choosing between the subjunctive (subjective, uncertain) and the indicative (objective, certain) depends heavily on the main verb and whether the sentence is affirmative, negative, or interrogative.

## 1. Certainty vs. Doubt
*   **Certainty** (takes indicative): *Je pense / crois / suis sûr qu'il **est** là.*
*   **Doubt** (takes subjunctive): *Je doute / il est douteux qu'il **soit** là.*

## 2. Negative and Interrogative Verbs of Opinion
When verbs of thinking or believing (*penser, croire, espérer*) are made negative or turned into a question, they shift from certainty to doubt and require the **subjunctive**:
*   *Je crois qu'il **a** raison.* (Indicative - affirmative opinion)
*   *Je ne crois pas qu'il **ait** raison.* (Subjunctive - negative opinion)
*   *Crois-tu qu'il **ait** raison ?* (Subjunctive - question showing doubt)
