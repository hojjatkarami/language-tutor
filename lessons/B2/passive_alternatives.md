# Alternatives to the Passive Voice (B2)

French speakers naturally avoid the passive voice (*La voiture est conduite par Marie*), preferring more dynamic structures.

## 1. Using 'On' (Active voice)
Instead of passive *Le pain est vendu ici*, use active **on**:
*   *Passive*: *Le vol a été annulé.* (The flight was canceled.)
*   *Alternative*: ***On** a annulé le vol.* (They/Someone canceled the flight.)

## 2. Pronominal/Reflexive Verbs
In French, things can "do actions to themselves" to express a passive state:
*   *Passive*: *Ce mot est prononcé ainsi.*
*   *Alternative*: *Ce mot **se prononce** ainsi.* (This word is pronounced this way - literally, *pronounces itself*.)
*   *Passive*: *Les fruits sont vendus au kilo.*
*   *Alternative*: *Les fruits **se vendent** au kilo.* (Fruits are sold by the kilo.)

## 3. Se faire + Infinitive
Used when someone gets something done to them (often negative or accidental):
*   *Passive*: *Il a été mordu par un chien.*
*   *Alternative*: *Il **se faire** mordre par un chien.* $\rightarrow$ *Il **s'est fait** mordre par un chien.* (He got bitten by a dog.)
