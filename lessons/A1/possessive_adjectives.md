# Possessive Adjectives (A1)

In French, possessive adjectives agree in **gender** and **number** with the **noun possessed**, not the owner.

## Possessive Adjectives Chart
| Owner | Masc. Sing. | Fem. Sing. | Plural |
|---|---|---|---|
| I (je) | **mon** | **ma** | **mes** |
| You (tu) | **ton** | **ta** | **tes** |
| He/She (il/elle) | **son** | **sa** | **ses** |
| We (nous) | **notre** | **notre** | **nos** |
| You (vous) | **votre** | **votre** | **vos** |
| They (ils/elles) | **leur** | **leur** | **leurs** |

## Special Rule for Feminine Nouns with Vowels
If a feminine singular noun starts with a **vowel** or **silent h**, use the **masculine** possessive adjective (**mon, ton, son**) to prevent a hiatus (clashing vowel sounds):
*   *ma amie* (Incorrect) $\rightarrow$ ***mon** amie* (Correct)
*   *sa école* (Incorrect) $\rightarrow$ ***son** école* (Correct)
