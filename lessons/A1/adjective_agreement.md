# Adjective Agreement and Placement (A1)

In French, adjectives must agree in **gender** (masculine/feminine) and **number** (singular/plural) with the nouns they modify.

## Forming the Feminine and Plural
*   **Feminine**: Usually formed by adding an **-e** to the masculine singular form.
    *   *un ami intelligent* (a smart friend) $\rightarrow$ *une amie intelligent**e***
*   **Plural**: Usually formed by adding an **-s** to the singular form.
    *   *les livres vert**s*** (the green books) $\rightarrow$ *les tables vert**es*** (the green tables)

## Exceptions
*   If a masculine adjective already ends in **-e**, it does not change in the feminine: *un garçon calme* $\rightarrow$ *une fille calme*.
*   Irregular changes (e.g., *beau* $\rightarrow$ *belle*, *nouveau* $\rightarrow$ *nouvelle*).

## Placement
Most French adjectives go **after** the noun:
*   *un livre intéressant* (an interesting book)
*   *une voiture rouge* (a red car)

However, a small group of common, short adjectives go **before** the noun (BAGS: Beauty, Age, Goodness, Size):
*   *une **belle** fleur* (a beautiful flower)
*   *un **petit** chat* (a small cat)
