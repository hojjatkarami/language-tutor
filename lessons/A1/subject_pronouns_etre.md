# Subject Pronouns & The Verb Être (A1)

In French, subject pronouns are essential because verbs conjugate differently for each one. The verb **être** (to be) is one of the most important verbs in French.

## Subject Pronouns
*   **Je** (I)
*   **Tu** (You - informal/singular)
*   **Il / Elle / On** (He / She / One or We informal)
*   **Nous** (We)
*   **Vous** (You - formal or plural)
*   **Ils / Elles** (They - masculine or feminine)

## Conjugation of Être (Present Tense)
| Pronoun | Conjugation | English |
|---|---|---|
| Je | **suis** | I am |
| Tu | **es** | You are |
| Il / Elle / On | **est** | He / She / One is |
| Nous | **sommes** | We are |
| Vous | **êtes** | You are |
| Ils / Elles | **sont** | They are |

## Example Sentences
*   *Tu es très gentil.* (You are very kind.)
*   *Elle est fatiguée.* (She is tired.)
*   *Ils sont en vacances.* (They are on vacation.)
