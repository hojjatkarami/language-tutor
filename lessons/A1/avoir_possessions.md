# The Verb Avoir & Expressions of Possession (A1)

The verb **avoir** (to have) is highly irregular and is used to talk about possession, age, and physical states.

## Conjugation of Avoir (Present Tense)
| Pronoun | Conjugation | English |
|---|---|---|
| J' | **ai** | I have |
| Tu | **as** | You have |
| Il / Elle / On | **a** | He / She / One has |
| Nous | **avons** | We have |
| Vous | **avez** | You have |
| Ils / Elles | **ont** | They have |

*Note: 'Je' contracts to 'J'' before a vowel sound.*

## Idiomatic Expressions with Avoir
In French, many states that use "to be" in English use **avoir** instead:
*   *Avoir faim* (To be hungry - literally, 'to have hunger')
*   *Avoir soif* (To be thirsty)
*   *Avoir chaud / froid* (To be hot / cold)
*   *Avoir ___ ans* (To be ___ years old)
