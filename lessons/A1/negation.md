# Basic Negation (ne... pas) (A1)

To make a sentence negative in French, place **ne** before the conjugated verb and **pas** after it.

## Basic Structure
> **Subject + ne + Verb + pas**

*   *Je parle.* (I speak.) $\rightarrow$ *Je **ne** parle **pas**.* (I do not speak.)
*   *Nous voulons.* (We want.) $\rightarrow$ *Nous **ne** voulons **pas**.* (We do not want.)

## Contraction before Vowels
If the verb starts with a vowel or a silent **h**, **ne** contracts to **n'**:
*   *J'aime.* (I like.) $\rightarrow$ *Je **n'**aime **pas**.* (I do not like.)
*   *Il habite.* (He lives.) $\rightarrow$ *Il **n'**habite **pas**.* (He does not live.)

## Change of Indefinite Articles
In a negative sentence, the indefinite articles (**un**, **une**, **des**) change to **de** (or **d'** before a vowel):
*   *J'ai un chien.* (I have a dog.) $\rightarrow$ *Je n'ai pas **de** chien.* (I don't have a dog.)
