# Asking Questions (A1)

In French, there are three primary structures used to form questions.

## 1. Intonation (Informal)
Keep the sentence structure exactly the same, but raise your voice at the end.
*   *Tu parles français.* (You speak French.) $\rightarrow$ *Tu parles français ?* (You speak French?)

## 2. Est-ce que (Standard)
Place **est-ce que** (literally "is it that") at the beginning of the statement.
*   *Tu parles français.* $\rightarrow$ ***Est-ce que** tu parles français ?* (Do you speak French?)
*   *Note: Contracts to **est-ce qu'** before a vowel: Est-ce qu'il parle français ?*

## 3. Inversion (Formal)
Swap the subject pronoun and the verb, connecting them with a hyphen.
*   *Tu parles français.* $\rightarrow$ ***Parles-tu** français ?* (Do you speak French?)

## Question Words
Add question words to the front of any of these patterns:
*   *Comment* (How) $\rightarrow$ *Comment est-ce que tu t'appelles ?*
*   *Où* (Where) $\rightarrow$ *Où habitez-vous ?*
