# Le Passé Composé with Avoir (A2)

The **passé composé** is the most common tense used to express actions completed in the past. When using the auxiliary verb **avoir**, the sentence structure is:

**Subject + Conjugated form of Avoir (present tense) + Past Participle**

## Form of Auxiliary Verb Avoir

*   Je **ai** (J'ai)
*   Tu **as**
*   Il / Elle / On **a**
*   Nous **avons**
*   Vous **avez**
*   Ils / Elles **ont**

## Forming the Past Participle

How you form the past participle depends on the infinitive verb ending:

1.  **-er verbs** (replace -er with **-é**):
    *   *parler* -> parl**é**
    *   *visiter* -> visit**é**
2.  **-ir verbs** (replace -ir with **-i**):
    *   *finir* -> fin**i**
    *   *choisir* -> chois**i**
3.  **-re verbs** (replace -re with **-u**):
    *   *vendre* -> vend**u**
    *   *attendre* -> attend**u**

## Examples

*   *J'ai visité le musée hier.* (I visited the museum yesterday.)
*   *Nous avons fini nos devoirs.* (We finished our homework.)
*   *Ils ont vendu leur maison.* (They sold their house.)

> [!NOTE]
> Unlike verbs that use *être* as an auxiliary, past participles using *avoir* **do not** agree in gender and number with the subject.
> *   *Elle a parlé.* (No extra 'e' added to 'parlé').
