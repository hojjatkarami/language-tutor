# The Near Future (Futur Proche) (A2)

The **futur proche** is used to talk about actions that are going to happen soon, or plans that are relatively certain. It corresponds to the English construction "going to + verb".

## Formation Formula

**Subject + Conjugated Aller (present tense) + Infinitive Verb**

## Conjugation of Aller (to go)
*   Je **vais**
*   Tu **vas**
*   Il / Elle / On **va**
*   Nous **allons**
*   Vous **allez**
*   Ils / Elles **vont**

## Examples

*   *Je **vais manger**.* (I am going to eat.)
*   *Nous **allons partir** ce soir.* (We are going to leave this evening.)
*   *Ils **vont voyager** en France.* (They are going to travel to France.)

> [!TIP]
> To form the negative, place **ne ... pas** around the conjugated verb *aller*.
> *   *Je **ne** vais **pas** manger.* (I am not going to eat.)
