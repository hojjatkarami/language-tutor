# The Pronouns Y and En (A2)

The pronouns **y** and **en** replace nouns introduced by specific prepositions to keep sentences shorter.

## 1. The Pronoun 'Y'
Replaces a place, or a noun introduced by the preposition **à** (excluding people):
*   *Je vais **à Paris**.* $\rightarrow$ *J'**y** vais.* (I am going there.)
*   *Elle pense **à son avenir**.* $\rightarrow$ *Elle **y** pense.* (She is thinking about it.)

## 2. The Pronoun 'En'
Replaces a noun introduced by **de**, a partitive article (du, de la, des), or a quantity:
*   *Je veux **du café**.* $\rightarrow$ *J'**en** veux.* (I want some.)
*   *Il a **trois enfants**.* $\rightarrow$ *Il **en** a trois.* (He has three of them.)
*   *Tu parles **de ton travail**.* $\rightarrow$ *Tu **en** parles.* (You speak of it.)

## Placement
Place them **before the conjugated verb** (or before the infinitive if there is one).
