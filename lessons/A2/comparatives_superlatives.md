# Comparatives and Superlatives (A2)

Comparatives and superlatives allow you to compare people, things, and actions.

## 1. Comparatives
Used to compare adjectives:
*   **Superiority**: *plus* + [adjective] + *que* (more... than)
    *   *Le train est **plus** rapide **que** la voiture.*
*   **Inferiority**: *moins* + [adjective] + *que* (less... than)
    *   *Le vélo est **moins** rapide **que** le train.*
*   **Equality**: *aussi* + [adjective] + *que* (as... as)
    *   *Jean est **aussi** intelligent **que** Pierre.*

## 2. Superlatives
Expresses "the most" or "the least" by placing the definite article (**le, la, les**) before the comparative:
*   *Le plus* (the most) $\rightarrow$ *C'est le train **le plus** rapide.*
*   *Le moins* (the least) $\rightarrow$ *C'est la voiture **la moins** chère.*

## Irregular Forms
*   *bon* (good) $\rightarrow$ comparative: ***meilleur** (better)*, superlative: ***le meilleur** (the best)*
