# Indirect Object Pronouns (A2)

Indirect object pronouns replace nouns representing people preceded by the preposition **à** (to). They answer the question "to whom?".

## Pronouns List
*   Me / M' (to me)
*   Te / T' (to you)
*   **Lui** (to him / to her)
*   Nous (to us)
*   Vous (to you plural/formal)
*   **Leur** (to them)

*Note: Unlike direct pronouns, there is no distinction of gender for 'lui' (both him and her) and no contraction for 'lui' or 'leur' before a vowel.*

## Examples
*   *Je parle **à mon père**.* $\rightarrow$ *Je **lui** parle.* (I speak to him.)
*   *Elle écrit **à ses amies**.* $\rightarrow$ *Elle **leur** écrit.* (She writes to them.)
