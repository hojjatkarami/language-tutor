# Prepositions with Geographical Names (A2)

In French, the preposition you use for "in" or "to" depends on the gender and number of the country or city.

## 1. Cities
Always use **à**:
*   *Je vais **à** Paris.* (I am going to Paris.)
*   *J'habite **à** Tokyo.* (I live in Tokyo.)

## 2. Feminine Countries & Continents
Countries ending in **-e** (and all continents) are feminine. Use **en**:
*   *Je vais **en** France.* (I go to France.)
*   *J'habite **en** Afrique.* (I live in Africa.)
*   *Note: Masculine countries starting with a vowel also use en: **en** Iran.*

## 3. Masculine Countries
Countries not ending in **-e** are masculine. Use **au**:
*   *Je vais **au** Canada.*
*   *J'habite **au** Mexique.* (Mexique is a rare masculine country ending in -e)

## 4. Plural Countries
Use **aux**:
*   *Je vais **aux** Pays-Bas.* (Netherlands)
*   *J'habite **aux** États-Unis.*
