# Direct Object Pronouns (A2)

Direct object pronouns replace nouns that receive the action of the verb directly, without a preposition. They answer the question "who?" or "what?".

## Pronouns List
*   Me / M' (me)
*   Te / T' (you singular)
*   **Le / La / L'** (him / her / it)
*   Nous (us)
*   Vous (you plural/formal)
*   **Les** (them)

## Placement
In simple tenses, the direct object pronoun goes **immediately before** the conjugated verb:
*   *Je vois **le livre**.* $\rightarrow$ *Je **le** vois.* (I see it.)
*   *Il aime **Marie**.* $\rightarrow$ *Il **l'**aime.* (He loves her.)

In double-verb structures (like the near future), it goes **before the infinitive**:
*   *Je vais regarder **le film**.* $\rightarrow$ *Je vais **le** regarder.* (I'm going to watch it.)
