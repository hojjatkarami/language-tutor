# The Imperfect Tense (L'Imparfait) (A2)

The **imparfait** is used to set the scene, describe habits, ongoing actions, or states of mind in the past. It translates to "was/were doing" or "used to do".

## How to Form the Imparfait
1.  Take the **Nous** form of the verb in the present tense.
    *   *parler* $\rightarrow$ *nous parlons* $\rightarrow$ stem is **parl-**
2.  Add the imparfait endings:
    *   Je: **-ais**
    *   Tu: **-ais**
    *   Il/Elle/On: **-ait**
    *   Nous: **-ions**
    *   Vous: **-iez**
    *   Ils/Elles: **-aient**

## Example (parler):
*   *Je parlais* (I was speaking)
*   *Nous parlions* (We were speaking)

## The Only Irregular Verb: Être
The stem for *être* is **ét-**:
*   *J'étais, tu étais, il était, nous étions, vous étiez, ils étaient*
