# Demonstrative Pronouns (B1)

Demonstrative pronouns replace a previously mentioned noun to indicate which specific one(s) we are referring to. They must agree in gender and number.

## Forms of Demonstrative Pronouns
| Gender/Number | Simple Form | With Suffix (-ci / -là) |
|---|---|---|
| Masc. Sing. | **celui** (the one) | **celui-ci** (this one) / **celui-là** (that one) |
| Fem. Sing. | **celle** (the one) | **celle-ci** (this one) / **celle-là** (that one) |
| Masc. Plur. | **ceux** (the ones) | **ceux-ci** (these ones) / **ceux-là** (those ones) |
| Fem. Plur. | **celles** (the ones) | **celles-ci** (these ones) / **celles-là** (those ones) |

## Usage
The simple forms (**celui, celle, etc.**) cannot stand alone. They must be followed by:
1.  A relative clause (starting with *qui, que, dont*):
    *   *J'aime **celui qui** est rouge.* (I like the one that is red.)
2.  A prepositional phrase (usually starting with *de*):
    *   *Mon stylo et **celui de** Pierre.* (My pen and Pierre's.)
