# Relative Pronouns (qui, que, dont, où) (B1)

Relative pronouns link two clauses together, referring back to a noun called the antecedent.

## 1. Qui (Subject)
Replaces the subject of the following verb. It can represent people or things:
*   *L'homme **qui** chante est mon oncle.* (The man **who** is singing...)
*   *Le livre **qui** est sur la table.* (The book **which** is...)

## 2. Que (Direct Object)
Replaces the direct object of the following verb:
*   *Le livre **que** je lis.* (The book [that] I am reading.)
*   *Note: Contracts to **qu'** before a vowel.*

## 3. Dont (Object of 'de')
Replaces a noun introduced by the preposition **de**:
*   *Je parle **de** ce film.* $\rightarrow$ *Le film **dont** je parle.* (The film **about which** I speak.)
*   *C'est l'ami **dont** le père est médecin.* (Whose father...)

## 4. Où (Place or Time)
Refers to a location or a moment in time:
*   *La ville **où** je suis né.* (The town **where** I was born.)
*   *Le jour **où** il est parti.* (The day **when** he left.)
