# Complex Negations (B1)

Beyond *ne... pas*, French has several complex negative expressions that replace *pas* to specify the type of negation.

## Main Negations
*   **ne... plus** (no longer, not anymore):
    *   *Je **ne** veux **plus** manger.* (I don't want to eat anymore.)
*   **ne... jamais** (never):
    *   *Il **ne** voyage **jamais**.* (He never travels.)
*   **ne... rien** (nothing):
    *   *Je **ne** sais **rien**.* (I know nothing.)
*   **ne... personne** (nobody):
    *   *Je **ne** vois **personne**.* (I see nobody.)

## Word Order in Compound Tenses (e.g., Passé Composé)
*   **plus, jamais, rien** go **between** the auxiliary and the past participle:
    *   *J'ai rien vu* (Incorrect) $\rightarrow$ *Je n'ai **rien** vu.*
*   **personne** goes **after** the past participle:
    *   *Je n'ai vu **personne**.*
