# The Gerund (Le Gérondif) (B1)

The **gérondif** is used to describe two actions happening at the same time, or to express how something is done (manner/cause). It corresponds to "while doing" or "by doing".

## How to Form the Gérondif
The structure is:
> **en** + **present participle**

To form the present participle:
1.  Take the **Nous** form of the present tense.
    *   *parler* $\rightarrow$ *nous parlons* $\rightarrow$ stem is **parl-**
2.  Add the ending **-ant**.
    *   *parlant*
3.  Add **en** before it: ***en parlant***.

### Example Verbs:
*   *finir* $\rightarrow$ *en finissant* (while finishing)
*   *faire* $\rightarrow$ *en faisant* (by doing)
*   *prendre* $\rightarrow$ *en prenant* (while taking)

## Irregular Forms
*   Être $\rightarrow$ **en étant**
*   Avoir $\rightarrow$ **en ayant**
*   Savoir $\rightarrow$ **en sachant**
