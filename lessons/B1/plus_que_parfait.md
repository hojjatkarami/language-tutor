# The Pluperfect Tense (Le Plus-que-parfait) (B1)

The **plus-que-parfait** (pluperfect) expresses an action in the past that occurred **before** another past action. It corresponds to the English "had + past participle".

## How to Form the Plus-que-parfait
It is a compound tense constructed like the passé composé, but the auxiliary verb is in the **imparfait**:

> **Auxiliary (avoir/être in Imparfait) + Past Participle**

### Examples:
*   *manger* (uses *avoir*):
    *   *J'**avais** mangé* (I had eaten)
    *   *Nous **avions** mangé* (We had eaten)
*   *partir* (uses *être* - requires agreement):
    *   *Elle **était** partie* (She had left)
    *   *Ils **étaient** partis* (They had left)
