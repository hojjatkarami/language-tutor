# The Passive Voice (La Voix Passive) (B1)

In the passive voice, the subject receives the action rather than performing it. The performer of the action is introduced by the preposition **par** (by).

## How to Form the Passive Voice
Use the conjugated verb **être** (in whatever tense is required) followed by the **past participle** of the main verb:

> **Subject + Être (conjugated) + Past Participle + par + Agent**

*Note: The past participle MUST always agree in gender and number with the subject.*

### Examples in Different Tenses:
*   **Present**: *La lettre **est écrite** par Pierre.* (The letter is written by Pierre.)
*   **Passé Composé**: *La maison **a été détruite** par le feu.* (The house was destroyed by fire.)
*   **Future**: *Les résultats **seront publiés** demain.* (The results will be published tomorrow.)
