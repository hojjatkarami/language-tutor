# The Simple Future (Le Futur Simple) (B1)

The **futur simple** corresponds to the English "will + verb". It is used for future plans, predictions, and formal statements.

## How to Form the Futur Simple
For regular verbs, use the **infinitive** as the stem. (For -re verbs, drop the final **-e**).
Add the following endings (which look like the present tense of *avoir*):
*   Je: **-ai**
    *   *Je parlerai* (I will speak)
*   Tu: **-as**
    *   *Tu parleras* (You will speak)
*   Il/Elle/On: **-a**
    *   *Il parlera* (He will speak)
*   Nous: **-ons**
    *   *Nous parlerons* (We will speak)
*   Vous: **-ez**
    *   *Vous parlerez* (You will speak)
*   Ils/Elles: **-ont**
    *   *Ils parleront* (They will speak)

## Irregular Stems
Some essential verbs have irregular future stems, but they use the same endings:
*   Être $\rightarrow$ **ser-** (*je serai*)
*   Avoir $\rightarrow$ **aur-** (*j'aurai*)
*   Aller $\rightarrow$ **ir-** (*j'irai*)
*   Faire $\rightarrow$ **fer-** (*je ferai*)
