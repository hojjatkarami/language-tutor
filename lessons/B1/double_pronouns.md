# Double Object Pronouns (B1)

When a sentence contains both a direct and an indirect object pronoun, they must follow a strict order.

## Pronoun Order Chart
In declarative sentences (before the verb), follow this order from left to right:

| 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | Verb |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **me**<br>**te**<br>**se**<br>**nous**<br>**vous** | **le**<br>**la**<br>**les** | **lui**<br>**leur** | **y** | **en** | [Verb] |

### Examples:
*   *Il donne le livre à moi.* $\rightarrow$ *Il **me le** donne.* (Me is Col 1, le is Col 2).
*   *Elle donne la lettre à lui.* $\rightarrow$ *Elle **la lui** donne.* (La is Col 2, lui is Col 3).
*   *Je donne des pommes à eux.* $\rightarrow$ *Je **leur en** donne.* (Leur is Col 3, en is Col 5).
