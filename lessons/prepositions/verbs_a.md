# Verbs Requiring the Preposition à

In French, many verbs are followed by the preposition **à** before a noun or an infinitive. These must be memorized as verb-preposition collocations.

## 1. Verbs followed by **à** + noun
- **penser à** (to think about someone/something)
  - *Example*: Je pense **à** mon avenir. (I am thinking about my future.)
- **s'intéresser à** (to be interested in)
  - *Example*: Elle s'intéresse **à** l'art. (She is interested in art.)
- **s'habituer à** (to get used to)
  - *Example*: Je m'habitue **à** ma nouvelle vie. (I am getting used to my new life.)

## 2. Verbs followed by **à** + infinitive
- **commencer à** (to start doing something)
  - *Example*: Il commence **à** parler. (He is starting to speak.)
- **réussir à** (to succeed in doing something)
  - *Example*: Nous avons réussi **à** finir le projet. (We succeeded in finishing the project.)
- **aider à** (to help to do something)
  - *Example*: Peux-tu m'aider **à** faire les devoirs ? (Can you help me do the homework?)
- **apprendre à** (to learn to do something)
  - *Example*: Elle apprend **à** conduire. (She is learning to drive.)
