# Geographic Prepositions: en, au, aux, à

In French, prepositions used for saying "to" or "in" a geographic location change based on the grammatical gender and number of the destination.

## 1. Cities: always use **à**
- No matter the gender of the city name, use **à**.
  - *Example*: Je vais **à** Montréal. (I am going to Montreal.)
  - *Example*: Il habite **à** Londres. (He lives in London.)

## 2. Feminine Countries & Continents: use **en**
- Countries ending in **-e** are feminine (except Mexico, Cambodia, Mozambique, Zimbabwe). Continents also use **en**.
  - *Example*: Elle habite **en** France. (She lives in France.)
  - *Example*: Voyager **en** Europe. (To travel in Europe.)
  - *Example*: Aller **en** Chine. (To go to China.)

## 3. Masculine Countries starting with a Consonant: use **au**
- Countries not ending in **-e** are generally masculine.
  - *Example*: Je vais **au** Canada. (I am going to Canada.)
  - *Example*: Il habite **au** Maroc. (He lives in Morocco.)

## 4. Plural Countries: use **aux**
- Countries that are plural in form.
  - *Example*: Nous allons **aux** États-Unis. (We are going to the United States.)
  - *Example*: Habiter **aux** Pays-Bas. (To live in the Netherlands.)

## 5. Masculine Countries starting with a Vowel: use **en**
- If a masculine country starts with a vowel, use **en** to avoid double vowels.
  - *Example*: Aller **en** Iran. (To go to Iran.)
  - *Example*: Vivre **en** Angola. (To live in Angola.)
