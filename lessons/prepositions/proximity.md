# Proximity and Distance: près de, loin de, à côté de

These compound prepositions require the preposition **de** (which contracts with definite articles as usual: `de + le = du`, `de + les = des`).

## 1. **près de** ("near" / "close to")
- Expresses proximity.
  - *Example*: J'habite **près de** la gare. (I live near the train station.)
  - *Example*: Il est assis **près du** feu. (He is sitting near the fire.)

## 2. **loin de** ("far from")
- Expresses distance.
  - *Example*: L'école est **loin de** ma maison. (The school is far from my house.)
  - *Example*: Nous sommes **loin des** yeux, mais près du cœur. (We are far from sight, but close to the heart.)

## 3. **à côté de** ("next to" / "beside")
- Expresses that something is immediately adjacent.
  - *Example*: Le musée est **à côté du** parc. (The museum is next to the park.)
  - *Example*: Assieds-toi **à côté de** moi. (Sit next to me.)

## 4. **en face de** ("opposite" / "facing")
- Expresses that something is directly across from.
  - *Example*: Le magasin est **en face de** la boulangerie. (The shop is opposite the bakery.)
