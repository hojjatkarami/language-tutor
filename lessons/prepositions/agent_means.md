# Agency, Means, and Purpose: par, avec, sans, pour

These prepositions describe how, with whom, or for what reason an action is completed.

## 1. **avec** ("with")
- Expresses companionship or using an instrument.
  - *Example*: Je mange **avec** mes amis. (I eat with my friends.)
  - *Example*: Écrire **avec** un stylo. (To write with a pen.)

## 2. **sans** ("without")
- Expresses the absence of something.
  - *Example*: Un café **sans** sucre, s'il vous plaît. (A coffee without sugar, please.)

## 3. **par** ("by" / "through")
- Expresses agency (by whom), means of transit (through), or distribution.
  - *Example*: Ce livre a été écrit **par** Victor Hugo. (This book was written by Victor Hugo.)
  - *Example*: Regarder **par** la fenêtre. (To look through the window.)
  - *Example*: Envoyer **par** e-mail. (To send by email.)

## 4. **pour** ("for" / "in order to")
- Expresses the destination/beneficiary of an item or the purpose of an action (followed by an infinitive).
  - *Example*: Ces fleurs sont **pour** toi. (These flowers are for you.)
  - *Example*: J'étudie **pour** réussir. (I study [in order] to succeed.)
