# Simple Directions: sur, sous, devant, derrière, entre

These simple prepositions allow you to locate objects relative to other physical structures.

## 1. **sur** ("on" / "upon")
- Indicates that something is touching and supported by a surface.
  - *Example*: Le chat dort **sur** le canapé. (The cat is sleeping on the sofa.)

## 2. **sous** ("under" / "underneath")
- Indicates that something is directly below another object.
  - *Example*: Les chaussures sont **sous** le lit. (The shoes are under the bed.)

## 3. **devant** ("in front of")
- Indicates position at the front of something.
  - *Example*: Attends-moi **devant** le restaurant. (Wait for me in front of the restaurant.)

## 4. **derrière** ("behind")
- Indicates position at the back or rear of something.
  - *Example*: Le jardin est **derrière** la maison. (The garden is behind the house.)

## 5. **entre** ("between")
- Used when indicating a position separating two things or people.
  - *Example*: La table est **entre** les deux chaises. (The table is between the two chairs.)
