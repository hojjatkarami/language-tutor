# Compound Prepositions: grâce à, à cause de, au lieu de

Compound prepositions are multi-word expressions that function as single prepositions.

## 1. **grâce à** vs **à cause de**
Both express cause, but with different connotations:
- **grâce à** ("thanks to") is used for positive causes.
  - *Example*: J'ai réussi **grâce à** ton aide. (I succeeded thanks to your help.)
- **à cause de** ("because of") is used for negative or neutral causes.
  - *Example*: Le vol est annulé **à cause de** la tempête. (The flight is cancelled because of the storm.)

## 2. **au lieu de** ("instead of")
- Indicates substitution or alternative.
  - *Example*: Prends du thé **au lieu de** café. (Have tea instead of coffee.)
  - *Example*: Travaille **au lieu de** dormir ! (Work instead of sleeping!)

## 3. **au-dessus de** vs **au-dessous de**
- **au-dessus de** means "above" or "higher than" (without physical contact).
  - *Example*: Le tableau est accroché **au-dessus du** canapé. (The picture is hung above the sofa.)
- **au-dessous de** means "below" or "lower than".
  - *Example*: La température est **au-dessous de** zéro. (The temperature is below zero.)
