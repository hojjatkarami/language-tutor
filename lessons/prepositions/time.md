# Prepositions of Time: avant, après, pendant, depuis, il y a

Prepositions of time are used to locate actions, durations, and events in relation to time.

## 1. **avant** vs **après**
- **avant** means "before".
  - *Example*: Je mange **avant** le dîner. (I eat before dinner.)
- **après** means "after".
  - *Example*: Nous partons **après** le film. (We leave after the movie.)

## 2. **depuis** ("since" / "for")
- Used to express an action that started in the past and is **still ongoing** in the present.
  - *Example*: J'habite ici **depuis** trois ans. (I have lived here for three years [and still do].)
  - *Example*: Il étudie **depuis** ce matin. (He has been studying since this morning.)

## 3. **pendant** ("during" / "for")
- Used to express a completed duration or a specific time period during which an action happened.
  - *Example*: Il a dormi **pendant** deux heures. (He slept for two hours [he is awake now].)
  - *Example*: J'ai lu un livre **pendant** le voyage. (I read a book during the trip.)

## 4. **il y a** ("ago")
- Combined with a time duration to indicate how long ago a completed action took place.
  - *Example*: Je suis arrivé **il y a** dix minutes. (I arrived ten minutes ago.)
