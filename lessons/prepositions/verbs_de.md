# Verbs Requiring the Preposition de

Just as some verbs require **à**, many others require the preposition **de** (or **d'** before a vowel) before a noun or an infinitive.

## 1. Verbs followed by **de** + noun
- **avoir besoin de** (to need)
  - *Example*: J'ai besoin **de** repos. (I need rest.)
- **parler de** (to talk about)
  - *Example*: Ils parlent **de** leur voyage. (They are talking about their trip.)
- **avoir peur de** (to be afraid of)
  - *Example*: Elle a peur **des** araignées. (She is afraid of spiders. *Note: de + les = des*)

## 2. Verbs followed by **de** + infinitive
- **essayer de** (to try to do something)
  - *Example*: Essaie **de** te concentrer. (Try to concentrate.)
- **décider de** (to decide to do something)
  - *Example*: Nous avons décidé **de** partir. (We decided to leave.)
- **refuser de** (to refuse to do something)
  - *Example*: Il refuse **de** répondre. (He refuses to answer.)
- **oublier de** (to forget to do something)
  - *Example*: N'oublie pas **de** fermer la porte. (Don't forget to close the door.)
- **arrêter de** (to stop doing something)
  - *Example*: Arrête **de** fumer. (Stop smoking.)
