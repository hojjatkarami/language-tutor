# Prepositions of Place: à, en, dans, chez

Prepositions of place describe where someone or something is located. In French, the choice of preposition depends on the type of space or destination.

## 1. The Preposition **à**
- Used to express "at", "in", or "to" for cities.
  - *Example*: Je vais **à** Paris. (I am going to Paris.)
  - *Example*: Il habite **à** Lyon. (He lives in Lyon.)
- Note: It contracts with definite articles: `à + le = au`, `à + les = aux`.
  - *Example*: Je vais **au** supermarché. (I am going to the supermarket.)

## 2. The Preposition **dans**
- Used to express "inside" or "in" a physical, enclosed space.
  - *Example*: Les clés sont **dans** le sac. (The keys are in the bag.)
  - *Example*: Il y a un livre **dans** la boîte. (There is a book in the box.)

## 3. The Preposition **en**
- Used with certain modes of transportation where you go inside (cars, trains, planes).
  - *Example*: Voyager **en** train. (To travel by train.)
- Also used for some feminine geographical locations or abstract concepts.
  - *Example*: Être **en** classe. (To be in class.)

## 4. The Preposition **chez**
- Used to mean "at/to the house of" or "at/to the business of" a person.
  - *Example*: Je suis **chez** Marie. (I am at Marie's house.)
  - *Example*: Je vais **chez** le médecin. (I am going to the doctor.)
